The proposed direct owner decision is:

Authorize one additional attempt and at most one corrective attempt for each, with the correction requiring recorded same-case prior evidence and an actual changed precondition or fix. Preserve every earlier result. Approve the revised glow reference-point oracle prospectively. Require explicit owner idle readiness before typing ACK launch and privately verified supported clipboard formats plus explicit owner idle readiness before a clipboard claim. No other case budget changes.

This text is a proposed decision for review, not evidence that the owner made it. An actual decision must name the selected cases, contracts, finite budgets and any exclusions. It may select fewer cases. No new case ID is proposed to bypass exhaustion.

Acceptance-only code preparation, source review and pure/injected verification are already authorized and may occur before cap approval; they need no additional permission. Additional live execution requires the direct cap decision and completion of those reviews/checks. Existing drivers must not be run with reset attempts or invented decision text. Their present retry guards intentionally reject an ordinary third attempt.

Keep the existing case IDs and historical attempt numbers. Any authorized supplemental run must record the real decision reference, `historicalAttemptsUsed: 2`, `continuationAttempt: 1` or `2`, and `lifetimeAttemptOrdinal: 3` or `4`, plus exact earlier receipt links. A decision-scoped ordinal is bookkeeping, never a new case identity or a rewritten old attempt. Validate the selected case and decision against its complete ledger before launch. The second supplemental attempt requires the first supplemental terminal `fail`/`blocked` receipt and a concrete correction; no automatic retry or fifth lifetime attempt is proposed. A launched `fail` or `blocked` consumes its supplemental attempt.
